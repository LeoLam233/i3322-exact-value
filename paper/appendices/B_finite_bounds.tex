\section{Finite rational bound certificates}\label{app:bounds}
The rational objects are \pkg{lower_d499.json} and
\pkg{upper_level4.json} in the canonical R1 component. They originate
in the immutable v2.0.0 release~\cite{Mghirbi2026data}; the original
license and attribution travel inside the supplied input archive.
The following specifies exactly what their independent checker proves.

\subsection{Lower witness}
The first object contains rational circle pairs \((c_i,s_i)\),
\(1\le i<499\), and rational amplitudes \(\lambda_i\),
\(1\le i\le499\). Set \(c_0=1,c_{499}=-1\). The finite predicates are
\(c_i^2+s_i^2=1\), \(s_i>0\), and \(N=\sum_i\lambda_i^2>0\).
Define
\[
 R_i^\pm=\frac12\begin{pmatrix}1-c_i&\pm s_i\\
                         \pm s_i&1+c_i\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 T_i^\pm=\frac12\begin{pmatrix}1+c_i&\pm s_i\\
                         \pm s_i&1-c_i\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 J=\frac12\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix}.
\]
The projectors in the target labels are the following direct sums.
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lll}
Operator & Two-dimensional blocks & Singleton\\ \hline
\(A_1,A_2\)&\(R_i^-,R_i^+\), even \(i=2,\ldots,498\)&\(1\) on \(e_1\)\\
\(A_3\)&\(J\), odd \(i=1,\ldots,497\)&\(1\) on \(e_{499}\)\\
\(B_1,B_2\)&\(T_i^-,T_i^+\), odd \(i=1,\ldots,497\)&\(0\) on \(e_{499}\)\\
\(B_3\)&\(J\), even \(i=2,\ldots,498\)&\(1\) on \(e_1\)
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Every block is symmetric and idempotent by the circle relation. The
checker additionally multiplies all six full sparse \(499\times499\)
matrices, checking symmetry and idempotency at every entry.
The state is \(N^{-1/2}\sum_i\lambda_ie_i\otimes e_i\).
For real matrices its expectation satisfies
\[
 \langle P\otimes Q\rangle
   =N^{-1}\sum_{i,j}\lambda_i\lambda_jP_{ij}Q_{ij},\qquad
 \langle P\otimes I\rangle=N^{-1}\sum_i\lambda_i^2P_{ii}.
\]
Direct contraction of \eqref{eq:bell}, including all zero coefficients,
is compared with the tridiagonal formula. The result is
\[
 v=N^{-1}\left(\sum_i d(c_{i-1},c_i)\lambda_i^2+
                       \sum_{i=1}^{498}s_i\lambda_i\lambda_{i+1}\right).
\]
The reduced numerator and denominator have 5,851 and 5,852 decimal
digits and are stored in \pkg{certificate/lower_receipt.json}.
Exact integer comparison, without a square-root approximation to
the state normalization, proves the interval for \(v\) in
Proposition~\ref{prop:R1}.

\subsection{Upper polynomial identity}
Generate all reduced single-party words in three involutions up to
length four, pair Alice and Bob words with total length at most four,
and order the 244 pairs by length and then lexicographically.
Adjacent equal letters cancel, and cross-party letters commute.
Adjoints reverse the order within each party. These reductions are
identities in every allowed operator representation.

On the ordered generator tuple \((X_1,X_2,X_3,Y_1,Y_2,Y_3)\), use
the signed substitutions
\[
 r=(-Y_1,-Y_2,Y_3,-X_2,-X_1,-X_3),\qquad
 s_0=(X_1,X_2,-X_3,Y_2,Y_1,Y_3).
\]
For each word-basis column and for signs
\((a,b)=(1,1),(1,-1),(-1,1),(-1,-1)\), form
\(\sum_{k=0}^3a^k(r^k+b\,s_0r^k)\) applied to that column.
Select the first linearly independent columns using rational
elimination, divide each by the positive gcd of its integer entries,
and preserve its sign. This constructs \(U_1,\ldots,U_4\) with
31, 30, 26 and 35 columns. The same operation on
\((1+s_0)(1-r^2)\) gives \(U_5\) with 61 columns; set \(U_6=rU_5\).
The construction only defines concrete integer matrices. No assertion
that they exhaust a symmetry decomposition is used.

The candidate gives five symmetric integer matrices \(M_k\) of sizes
31, 30, 26, 35 and 61, with common denominator
\[
 D_0=18534051411918436794453781905408000000000>0 .
\]
Exact symmetric fraction-free elimination checks positive leading
determinants \(\Delta_j\), with \(\Delta_0=1\), using the update
\[
 b'_{ij}=\frac{b_{kk}b_{ij}-b_{ik}b_{kj}}{\Delta_{k-1}}.
\]
Each division must have positive denominator and zero remainder.
The Schur-complement determinant identity shows inductively that
the pivots are leading principal determinants; the corresponding
congruent diagonal entries are \(\Delta_j/\Delta_{j-1}>0\).
Thus all five \(M_k\) are positive definite. The certificate stores
all 183 determinants.

Construct
\[
 Y=D_0^{-1}\left(\sum_{k=1}^4U_kM_kU_k^T+
                      U_5M_5U_5^T+U_6M_5U_6^T\right)\succeq0 .
\]
Real positivity follows term by term under congruence, and complex
positivity by real and imaginary parts. The full Gram numerator
is reconstructed in \pkg{certificate/reconstructed_gram.json}.
Expansion of every ordered product \(w_i^*w_j\), reduction as above,
and comparison at all 8,452 normal-form positions prove
\eqref{eq:upperidentity}. To pass from the finite identity to an
operator inequality, evaluate the words on a strategy and set
\(\eta=(w_i\xi)_i\). Then
\(\langle\xi,\mathbf w^*Y\mathbf w\,\xi\rangle
=\eta^*(Y\otimes I)\eta\ge0\).
This proves the uniform upper bound for every finite dimension.

\subsection{Acceptance and mutation contract}
Both candidates are parsed with duplicate-key and nonfinite-number
rejection; rational fields reject floating values and invalid
denominators. The normalization coefficient dictionaries must agree
exactly, the deterministic local maximum must be zero, all lower
projector and value checks must pass, all upper determinant and
coefficient checks must pass, and the rational endpoint comparisons
must hold. Each condition fails by an explicit exception. Fresh
receipts are compared with the frozen ones only after recomputation.
R1's 13 mutation probes test mathematical rejection paths; original
candidate generation is not rerun and is not a proof premise.
