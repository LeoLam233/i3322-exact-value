\begin{abstract}
We present a machine-assisted exact characterization of the supremum
\(\St\) of a specified normalization of \(I_{3322}\), over binary projective
measurements on arbitrary finite-dimensional tensor products, with no fixed
bound on local dimension. A selected positive Jacobi carrier at the supremum
has strictly decreasing labels and prescribed outer tails. Its stationary
recurrence converts the problem into full-state matching of a one-dimensional
unstable tail and a one-dimensional stable tail. A complete interval-certified
analysis with two independent phases excludes asymmetric full matches before
reducing to an explicitly defined analytic scalar residual. That residual has
a strictly positive derivative throughout the full admissible parameter
interval and exactly one zero. Consequently \(\St\) is the unique matching
parameter, with a strict rational enclosure of width
\(4.60932356071910243385112719549938850842\times10^{-52}\).
The proof separates analytic arguments, finite certificates, and external
theorem premises. It uses three specified interfaces from Coladangelo's v1
preprint and the standard real-analytic stable/unstable manifold theorem.
It makes no commuting-operator equality claim. The frozen proof survives
internal reconstruction, exact replay, and adversarial and provenance audits;
a non-load-bearing midpoint-helper defect remains disclosed. External expert
human validation and journal peer review of this exact-value result have not
yet occurred.
\end{abstract}
