\section{Limitations, validation status and disclosure}\label{sec:validation}
The mathematical chain is closed relative to the explicit external
premises and finite arithmetic contracts stated above. The frozen proof
package survives internal clean reconstruction, independent adversarial
checking of specified components, exact replay, and the project-level
source-provenance audit. This describes the recorded scope of checking.
It does not turn model agreement into independent mathematical evidence.
External expert human validation of the new exact-value theorem has
\emph{not yet occurred}, and journal peer review of this new result has
\emph{not yet occurred}.

The final integrated adversarial-audit verdict is
\pkg{OPEN-MINOR}. Its sole remaining mathematical-package issue is M001:
the generic Decimal midpoint helper can return a point outside an
arbitrarily narrow input interval. For example, at precision 90 its
midpoint of the point interval \(1-10^{-90}\) can be \(1\).
The defect remains genuine and unpatched in the frozen source.
The sealed arithmetic audit reports that all 22,074 actual midpoint
returns in the submitted traces lay within their respective input
intervals, and that no submitted load-bearing enclosure was invalidated.
The assembly's independent rational integration replay checked the 13
actual root-Newton centers. It did not repeat the historical
107,716,343-operation observer audit. Thus M001 is retained as an open,
non-load-bearing implementation defect, not relabelled as nonexistent.

The project source-provenance verdict is
\pkg{SOURCE-PROVENANCE-OPEN-MINOR}. It preserves the known source-text
errors, M001, and the unresolved complete historical generation ancestry
of the R1 witnesses. According to the frozen audits none of these
remaining issues is load-bearing for the theorem. They nevertheless
belong in the validation record and should be available to human referees.

The theorem concerns only the specified finite-dimensional projective
tensor supremum. It makes no commuting=tensor assertion, uses no R2,
and does not claim uniqueness of a physical optimizer or carrier,
finite-dimensional attainment, or an elementary closed form for the value.
