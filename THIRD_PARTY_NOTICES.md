# Third-party and frozen-material notices

The MIT and CC BY 4.0 grants in LICENSE apply only to material authored by the owner. Third-party materials and pre-existing sealed/frozen artifacts are not automatically relicensed. Their existing notices, rights and licenses remain controlling, including where no affirmative license is present. A citation, public availability or inclusion in a proof package does not establish permission to relicense the cited work.

The canonical closure, hostile audits, source-provenance records, rejected-branch evidence, earlier manuscript archive and embedded reproducibility/input archives retain their original bytes and legal status. New packaging, public metadata and this licensing map do not alter those records. Retained notices inside nested archives must remain with the material to which they apply.

Examples of third-party material in the historical custody chain include the frozen Coladangelo source, Mghirbi's released rational witnesses, cited research sources, and the bundled SymPy 1.14.0 and mpmath 1.3.0 wheels. The existing notices and attribution in their respective packages govern; no new rights to these materials are asserted here. References to Pauwels, Pal-Vértesi, Cabré-Fontich-de la Llave and Shub are scholarly citations, not blanket redistribution grants.

The local CC BY 4.0 file is the unmodified standard legal text supplied with the installed pypdfium2 distribution. It is included as a generic license resource, not as project-specific input; no network retrieval was used. The MIT text is the standard unmodified license with the owner's copyright notice.

Sealed historical records contain inherited workstation/build paths intentionally preserved under the owner's disclosure decision. This does not change their licensing. No private chat transcript is added to the public package.
