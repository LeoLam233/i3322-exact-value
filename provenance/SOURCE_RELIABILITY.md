The frozen project source-provenance audit distinguishes internal reproof, independent data validation, remaining external theorems, software trust and historical context. Its classifications are evidence descriptions, not authority conferred by an AI label.

The audit records no state D/E dependency and no load-bearing source-text error. That is its scoped verdict; this repository assembly does not claim new independent expert validation. An unpublished source's status, author reputation, absence of an erratum, or use of another AI model is not a correctness certificate.

Version-specific comparisons in the audit are frozen historical findings. Later numerical bounds do not replace the frozen R1 endpoints. The standard-theorem audit records direct inspection of the analytic primary article and a failed full-textbook retrieval; it does not pretend both texts were inspected.
